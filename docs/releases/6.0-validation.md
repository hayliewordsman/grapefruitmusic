# Grapefruit Audio 6.0 — build 67

6.0 stable: 6.0 beta 1 and beta 2 (see their notes) and the two changes below, released without a
beta of their own as asked. Since 5.1: the rename to Grapefruit Audio, Audiobooks, playback speed,
lyrics timing, OPML, tab menus, the Music tab, the TV & Movies tab, the headphones icon and
wordmark, and the sleep timer.

## Sleep timer

- playback/SleepTimer: off; 5, 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes; or the end of the song, episode or
  audiobook chapter ("End of Book" for a book without chapters). Live streams and the FM radio offer
  the times only.
- Timed: one wait (Handler) until 8 seconds before, then 100 ms steps fading the player out
  (Player.setFade, a multiplier in applyVolume beside Volume Match and ducking) before it pauses
  and the volume is put back. It counts by SystemClock.elapsedRealtime (the time asleep included).
  The FM radio is switched off (Radio.stop; Radio.peek, so the timer never starts up a radio
  nothing has used).
- End of song or episode: Player's onCompletion asks the timer before moving on; if it's set, the
  next item is loaded but not played (or, at the queue's end, it stops as it would), and the timer
  is done. Repeat One doesn't hold it off.
- End of chapter: a file's end is a chapter's end (onCompletion, as above). Inside a file (an M4B's
  chapters) the timer looks once a second at where the book is; when it has played on into the next
  chapter (a few seconds' progress since the last look) it goes back to that chapter's start and
  pauses, so the book resumes with the new chapter. A chapter skipped to or chosen becomes the one
  to finish.
- Now Playing's ⋯ menu, on every kind of Now Playing, ends with Sleep Timer ("Sleep Timer: 23 min
  left", or the end it's waiting for), opening a list with the one set ticked. The ⋯ button is in the
  accent while it's set (TalkBack: "More actions, sleep timer on").
- The ⋯ menu was a song's or an episode's menu, or Add to Presets for the FM radio; for an audiobook
  or an internet station it did nothing. It's now one menu: a song's rows (TrackActions.collect, the
  same rows as before), an episode's (PodcastsFragment.episodeItems), a station's (Play Next, Add to
  Up Next, Add to/Remove from My Stations), a book's (Play from the Start), or the FM radio's (Add
  to Presets…), then Sleep Timer.
- Checked with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): the menu and the timer's list for a
  song, an internet station, an audiobook and a podcast episode, set and not; a 5-minute timer at
  half volume 4 seconds before, paused at 5:00 with the volume back at full; End of Song stops once
  (not for a live station).

## Radio's + and ✓

- Now Playing's + / ✓ buttons for the FM radio's presets and an internet station's My Stations
  show the icon alone (ActionIconView.setLabelVisible(false)): "Save" and "Saved" all but filled the
  52dp preset button. TalkBack still says Add to Presets, Remove from My Stations and so on. The
  station's button is padded to give its icon the size of the labelled buttons' beside it.

## The listing and the F-Droid repository

- fastlane: the title "Grapefruit Audio: Music, Podcasts, and Audiobooks" (49 of 50 characters),
  the summary "Music, podcasts and audiobooks: your files, Navidrome, Subsonic and Jellyfin" (76 of
  80), the description (audiobooks, speed, sleep timer, OPML, lyrics timing) and the icon with
  headphones (tools/brand/logo.py store_icon). These are what both F-Droid repositories show.
- Grapefruit's own F-Droid repository is now called "Grapefruit" (it carries Grapefruit Audio and
  Grapefruit TV), its archive "Grapefruit Archive"; its front page says Grapefruit Audio and shows
  the new stacked logo, 320px wide for its proportions. The repository's own icon (docs/icon.png)
  is unchanged.
- The official F-Droid recipe (fdroid/official) builds 6.0 from its release commit, with AutoName
  Grapefruit Audio and Binaries grapefruit-audio-%v.apk.
- The release workflow publishes 6.0 to the F-Droid repository, as a stable release.
