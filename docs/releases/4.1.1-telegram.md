🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 4.1.1

• Fixed: starting a podcast episode could close Grapefruit on Android 4.x tablets.
• Podcasts with ads added as they play (most big-network shows) now stay on one copy of the episode. Pausing, seeking or a dropped connection no longer makes an episode jump, repeat a bit or end in the wrong place.
• If Grapefruit ever closes unexpectedly, it keeps a short report and offers to share it the next time you open it. Nothing is sent unless you choose to.
