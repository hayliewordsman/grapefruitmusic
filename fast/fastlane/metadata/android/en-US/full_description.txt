Grapefruit FAST is a small, native app for free live TV on Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean) and later, from the makers of Grapefruit Music & Podcasts. No account, no subscription, nothing to sign in to.

• 59 free channels: news from Canada, the United States, the UK, Europe and Australia, and classic series, reality and lifestyle channels
• Captions where a channel provides them
• Favourites, search by name, city or genre, and Continue watching
• Full screen, with sideways-to-full-screen on phones
• Adaptive quality, with Data saver and Compatibility modes for slow connections and older devices
• Grapefruit Dark, Grapefruit Light and Pitch Black themes, in Grapefruit's floating pill design
• Phones, tablets, square screens and Android TV, with remote control support

Channels come straight from broadcasters and their official distributors. Some are offered only in certain countries; if a channel's provider doesn't offer it where you are, the player says so. The channels' own advertisements play as usual. Grapefruit FAST adds no ads of its own and no tracking.
