Add to F-Droid

Works with F-Droid and Droid-ify, on Android 6 to 13. Android 14 and newer can't install Grapefruit Music: it's made for older phones and tablets.

  1. Tap Add to F-Droid.
  2. Your app store asks to add Grapefruit's repository: tap Add.
  3. Search for Grapefruit Music and tap Install. Updates arrive there from then on.
Nothing happened?

You need F-Droid or Droid-ify on this phone first. Get F-Droid from f-droid.org, open it once, then come back and tap the button again.

Or add it by hand: in F-Droid, go to Settings › Repositories › + and paste this address.

Older phone or tablet?

F-Droid and Droid-ify need Android 6 or newer. On Android 4.2 to 5.1, download the app and open the file to install it (allow installing from unknown sources if your phone asks).
