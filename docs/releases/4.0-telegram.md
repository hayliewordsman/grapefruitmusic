🍊 Grapefruit Music 4.0

• Podcasts are here! Discover shows, subscribe, download episodes for offline listening, and follow download progress in the app and notifications. Subscribed shows are checked periodically for new episodes.
• Unified Search now finds music, internet radio stations and podcasts.
• A refreshed Now Playing layout adapts to tall, square and landscape screens, with sharper playback controls and icon-only actions: Lyrics, Karaoke, Shuffle, Repeat and Queue.
• Library artwork and navigation have been refreshed. Playlists now live in Library, keeping the bottom menu simpler.
• Rebuilt on-device stereo Karaoke Mode improves vocal reduction, especially for off-centre vocals. It stays lightweight and FOSS, with updated model and training credits in About. Results vary by recording; some vocals may remain and instruments can also be affected.
• Karaoke strength now has its own space beside the artwork.
• Clearer Settings arrows, more consistent spacing, and fixes for podcast playback and downloads on Jelly Bean.
• Android 4.2.2 support is retained, with updated support for modern Android.

Install the signed APK over your existing signed Grapefruit Music installation to keep your data. Failed podcast downloads from older betas can be retried after updating.
