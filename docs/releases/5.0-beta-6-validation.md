# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 6 — build 55

- Ui.PILL: a radius past any height (GradientDrawable keeps a radius to half the shorter side,
  on Android 4.2 too), so controls are pills whatever their height. Used for the mini player
  (64dp, was 18dp corners; its artwork is now round, concentric with the pill's end), tab bar,
  chips, buttons, pill buttons, text buttons, dialog buttons, the source picker, notices, Radio's
  genre buttons and a playlist cover's Edit label (was a band with 4dp corners).
- Text fields and search boxes (field_*.xml): 1000dp corners (pills), 16dp side padding; a
  playlist's description box (two to six lines) is a 22dp rounded box instead.
- Rows: Ui.rowBackground replaces gfRowBackground (row_*.xml, deleted): nothing, and a pill inset
  6dp from the row's ends while pressed. The inset drawable reports no padding, so rows keep their
  own. Cards (album, station and preset tiles) get Ui.cardBackground, a rounded square like their
  artwork. Unused pill_*.xml and gfPillBackground removed; the picture-grid selector's corners
  follow the artwork (14dp).
- ⋯ button (Ui.moreButton): its padding was set before its inset background, which replaced it
  with the 4dp inset, so the dots were drawn as wide as the circle; now set after (10dp). Playlist
  and album rows pulled the button 8dp into their padding, where it was clipped (ViewGroups clip
  to padding); the rows' right padding is now 10dp, as in the Library's song rows.
- Tag editor: Change/Add Artwork wraps its label (it took the vertical layout's full width);
  Cancel/Save bar padding 16dp; artwork corners as elsewhere (was 6dp). Station and preset tiles'
  artwork corners as elsewhere (was 8dp). Add Songs sheet: 24dp corners like the other sheets.
- Equalizer: the curve on a 24dp rounded card inset like the rows, instead of a full-width band
  with a separator.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): Library, Songs (and a pressed row),
  a playlist and an album, Now Playing, Search, Radio, Search Podcasts, Equalizer, a list dialog,
  an alert, a notice, Settings and the tag editor, in Grapefruit Dark and Light (portrait) and
  Pitch Black (landscape).
- Version 5.0-beta-6 (55): a prerelease, not published to the F-Droid repository.
- Local Gradle (JDK 21): assembleDebug, assembleRelease, testDebugUnitTest, lintDebug.
