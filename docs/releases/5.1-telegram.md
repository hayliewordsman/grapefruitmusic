🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.1

Sideways and square screens:
• Podcasts now matches the Library on a phone on its side or a tablet: Discover and Subscriptions show their podcasts as cards scrolling sideways beside the floating chips, with the title and note scrolling along. Downloads stays a list.
• Lyrics fill the whole left side where the artwork was, with the song's title and artist on the right above the progress bar. Up Next does the same.
• On narrow screens like a Unihertz Titan or a Clicks Communicator, the lyrics are sized to fit so a line holds a phrase, and Up Next leaves more room for song titles.

Fixes:
• The mini player: long titles scroll smoothly by, three times a song, instead of moving one letter and jumping back to the start.
• The playing notification: on Android 4.2 it could show only "Grapefruit" with no song. Its pictures are less than half the size they were (Android drops a notification that's too big without saying so), and even the first notification names the song.
• Adding a whole album to a playlist could close Grapefruit. Playlists on this device are now saved so a fault in the phone's media library can't take Grapefruit with it: you'll see a message instead.
• A crash now always leaves a report to share, even when the phone runs out of memory.

Also:
• The download is now called grapefruit-5.1.apk.
• Still runs on Android 4.2.2 and later; updates over 5.0 with the same signing key.
