# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 5 — build 54

- SectionMenu (new): one menu for a tab's sections. Title and search button, then a chip per
  section; the Library's has the source picker first (now a hairline pill with a chevron, set
  apart by a hairline, so the accent marks only the section showing). Pinned above the section
  in portrait and on square screens (a row that scrolls sideways, keeping its place and bringing
  the chosen chip into view); on a wide screen (RowAdapter.landscape) a fixed 260dp column with
  the chips stacked, beside the section.
- Library: Recently Added (LibraryFragment), Playlists, Artists, Albums and Songs
  (BrowseFragment.section, for the current source) under the same menu. Podcasts: Discover,
  Subscriptions and Downloads (PodcastsFragment.section). The "Recently Added" heading is gone
  (the chosen chip says it); Shuffle All and New Playlist stay at the top of their lists.
- MainActivity.showLibrary / showPodcasts: a chip replaces the page in place (no slide, nothing
  added to Back); the tab's sections are kept while it's open, so each keeps what it loaded and
  its scroll; the Library's are dropped when the source changes. Tapping the tab starts afresh.
  Back from a section returns to Recently Added or Discover. The download notification opens
  Podcasts' Downloads section.
- A section's albums, artists or shows open on top as before, with Back to the section.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed), which also tapped the chips and
  Back: portrait phone 360x760dp (dark and light), landscape phone 760x360dp, Unihertz Titan
  480x456dp, tablet 1024x600dp, small phone 320x533dp. Chip taps leave the back stack empty;
  Recently Added and Discover come back as the same page.
- Version 5.0-beta-5 (54): a prerelease, not published to the F-Droid repository.
- Local Gradle (JDK 21): assembleDebug, assembleRelease, testDebugUnitTest, lintDebug.
