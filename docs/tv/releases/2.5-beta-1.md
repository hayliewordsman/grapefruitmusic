# Grapefruit TV 2.5 beta 1

- Floating player: leave a channel (Back, ‹ or another tab) and it keeps playing in a small window that floats over the app. Drag it anywhere and it settles against the nearer side, clear of the tabs. Tap it to go back to the channel, or tap ✕ (or fling it off the side) to stop it. It works on every Android version Grapefruit TV supports, 4.2 and up.
- One channel at a time: tapping another channel stops the one playing and starts the new one. The floating player and the channel page share the one stream and pass it between them, so moving between them never reconnects or opens a second connection. That matters for IPTV services that limit simultaneous connections.
- While a channel floats, the floating player takes Continue watching's place. Keep Screen On still applies.
- Settings › Watching › Floating Player (on by default). With it off, leaving a channel stops it, as in 2.0.
- Playback still stops when you leave the app. Android's picture-in-picture and background play are planned for later 2.5 betas.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over 2.0.
