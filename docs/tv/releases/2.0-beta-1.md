# Grapefruit TV 2.0 beta 1

Grapefruit FAST is now Grapefruit TV: all your live TV in one place.

- Your sources: add an IPTV service you subscribe to, with its M3U playlist address or its server, username and password (Settings › Your Sources). Its channels appear under the Added chip, by name. Grapefruit TV doesn't provide, sell or recommend any service; only add ones you're entitled to use.
- Your TV Apps: the streaming and TV-provider apps on your device (Crave, Citytv+, Sling, Hulu, Paramount+, Fibe TV, Ignite TV, Xfinity, DIRECTV, DISH, Shaw and more) sit at the top of Live TV and open with a tap. Add App pins any other. You sign in and watch in those apps.
- The new name and wordmark on the splash screen, About page, side rail and TV banner.
- Live TV drops its tagline. Settings › Channels & Availability explains built-in channels, your sources and Your TV Apps.
- Channel names only; no programme guide.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over Grapefruit FAST 1.0.
