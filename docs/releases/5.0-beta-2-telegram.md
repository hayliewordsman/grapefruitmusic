🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 2

The new look on landscape and square screens:
• On a phone turned sideways, a tablet, or a square screen like the Unihertz Titan's or the Clicks Communicator's, the tabs are a rail down the left, so pages keep their full height. The mini player sits under the page.
• Now Playing has two columns there: the artwork on the left; the title, controls and buttons on the right. Shuffle, back, play, on and repeat get smaller to fit, down to a Titan Pocket.
• Square screens keep Library's up-and-down pages; it scrolls sideways only on wide screens, as before.
• Settings and its pages keep to a readable width on wide screens.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository.
