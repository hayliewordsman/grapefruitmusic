# Grapefruit FAST 1.0

The first stable release of Grapefruit FAST, free live TV in the Grapefruit family.

- 59 free live channels: news from Canada, the United States, the UK, Europe and Australia (including CBC News, Global News, CityNews, CPAC, BBC News, Sky News, DW News, ABC News Australia, NBC News NOW, CBS News 24/7, ABC News Live, CNN Headlines and Cheddar News), and classic series, reality and lifestyle channels.
- Every channel is listed wherever you are. If a channel's provider doesn't offer it in your location, the player says so on the picture.
- Captions where a channel provides them, following Android's caption setting until you choose.
- Favourites, search by name, city or genre, Continue watching, and full screen (turn a phone sideways while watching).
- Adaptive quality, with Data saver and Compatibility modes.
- Grapefruit Dark, Grapefruit Light and Pitch Black themes, Geist type and the Geist Pixel Triangle wordmark, in Grapefruit's floating pill design.
- Android 4.2.2 and later: phones, tablets, square screens and Android TV.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over the betas.

No account, subscription, tracking or extra ads. Channels' own advertisements and regional restrictions apply. Grapefruit is not affiliated with the listed broadcasters.
