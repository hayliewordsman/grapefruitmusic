🍊🎧 Grapefruit Audio 6.0

Grapefruit Music & Podcasts is now Grapefruit Audio: Music, Podcasts, and Audiobooks. Everything new since 5.1:

📚 Audiobooks
• A new Audiobooks tab for DRM-free audiobooks: M4B and M4A books with their chapters, MP3 books (one file with chapters, or a folder of parts), FLAC, Ogg and WAV.
• Libro.fm's downloads play as they are: tap Add and choose its zip of MP3s (it's unzipped for you) or its M4B. Books in the phone's Audiobooks folder show up by themselves.
• Each book remembers where you were, Continue Listening puts the one you're in at the top, and touching and holding a book plays it from the start, marks it finished or removes one you added.
• Now Playing for a book: the chapter as the title, Chapter 4 of 9 under it, 15-second skips, and Chapters in place of Up Next: tap one to go there.
• The Audiobooks tab suggests buying from Libro.fm: DRM-free audiobooks, and every purchase supports a local bookstore.

😴 Sleep timer
• In the ⋯ menu of every Now Playing: music, podcasts, audiobooks, internet radio and FM radio. Stop in 5, 10, 15, 30 or 45 minutes or an hour (it fades out gently), or at the end of the song, episode or chapter. The ⋯ button turns the accent colour while it's set.

⏩ Playback speed
• 0.8× to 2× with voices at their own pitch, set separately for music, podcasts and audiobooks (Speed in Now Playing). The time left counts down at the speed you listen.

🎤 Lyrics timing
• If a song's lyrics run ahead or behind, tap Timing in Lyrics and move them half a second earlier or later. Grapefruit remembers it for that song.

🎙️ Podcasts
• Import and Export your subscriptions (Podcasts › Subscriptions) with an OPML file, from or for another podcast app.

🧭 Tabs
• Tab menus: tap the tab you're on, or touch and hold any tab but Settings, for its sections, sources and searches. Search's menu picks what to search.
• Library is now Music, with a music-note icon, and Recently Added shows only music and playlists.
• TV & Movies: if Grapefruit TV & Movies (or Grapefruit TV) is installed, a TV & Movies tab opens it.

📻 Radio
• The + and ✓ buttons in radio Now Playing are icons alone, with no squeezed label under them. The ⋯ menu for an internet station now works (Play Next, Add to Up Next, My Stations, Sleep Timer).

🍊 A new look
• The grapefruit wears headphones, on the launcher, the splash screen and the About page.
• The name in Geist Pixel's triangles, on two lines, as large as the screen allows so the triangles show.
• The APK is now called grapefruit-audio-6.0.apk.

Also in 6.0: audiobooks never shuffle or repeat, and the mini player, notification and lock screen show a book's title.

Get it from Grapefruit's F-Droid repository or the GitHub release. It updates Grapefruit Music & Podcasts in place: your music, podcasts and settings stay.
