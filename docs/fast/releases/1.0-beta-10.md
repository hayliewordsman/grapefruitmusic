# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 10

- Settings › Channels › Test Channels: checks every channel from your device, wherever you are in the world, and lists the ones that play there. Channels that play are unlocked for your region, including Xumo's channels outside the United States when their streams work where you are.
- Share Results sends a plain-text report (your region, Android version and each channel's result) so it's easy to tell us what works where. It includes no device ID or IP address.
- Only the channels' own servers are contacted; nothing is sent anywhere else. A channel a provider refuses is locked again on the next test; a check that fails for another reason (a network hiccup) changes nothing.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over betas 3 to 9.
