# Grapefruit TV & Movies 3.0 beta 1

Grapefruit TV is now **Grapefruit TV & Movies**: a new wordmark, splash screen and name throughout the app. The app ID and APK name stay the same, so it installs over 2.x.

- **Movies:** a new tab of free films that are legal to watch: in the public domain in the United States, or released under Creative Commons licences by their makers. Film noir, comedies, silent classics, horror, westerns and animation, from His Girl Friday and Night of the Living Dead to the Blender Foundation's open movies and Sita Sings the Blues. They stream from the Internet Archive and can be downloaded to watch offline. Every film was picked by hand: nothing discriminatory.
- **On Demand:** when one of your sources offers films and series, an On Demand tab appears with Films, Series (seasons and episodes) and your service's own groups. Hold a film or an episode to **download** it for offline viewing. Downloads resume after a dropped connection, run on Wi-Fi only unless you change that, and are kept under Downloads.
- **Guide:** turn on the Guide chip in Live TV (or Settings › Guide in Live TV) to see each channel's programmes beside it. Drag sideways to move through the day. Programmes come from your source's guide when it publishes one; elsewhere the guide shows hour-long blocks on news channels and half-hour blocks on everything else, labelled "Program information currently unavailable". A channel's page shows what's on now and next.
- **Recordings (DVR):** record free channels and your IPTV channels on this device. Tap a programme in the guide, use Record on a channel's page, or use Schedule a Recording. The Recordings tab has All, Finished and Scheduled chips. Recordings start on time and carry on with the app in the background or swiped out of Recents: a notification shows while they run, and they're set again after a restart. A force stop in Android's settings cancels them until you next open the app.
- **Streams at a Time:** your services' stream limits are respected across watching, recording and downloading. A login's limit is read from the service; for a playlist you can set it under Settings › Your Sources. If a recording needs the stream you're watching, playback stops and tells you why.
- Films and recordings have a bar to move through them, Pause, back 10 s and forward 30 s, and pick up where you left off.
- The in-app floating player and the one-stream-at-a-time rule from 2.5 beta 1 cover films, episodes and recordings too. Background play is no longer planned.
- Encrypted streams, and streams that keep their sound separately, can't be recorded yet.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over 2.5 beta 1 and 2.0.
