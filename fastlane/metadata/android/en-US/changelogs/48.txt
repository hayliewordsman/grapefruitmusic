Podcasts with ads added as they play stay on one copy of the episode: pausing, seeking or a dropped connection no longer makes it jump, repeat or end in the wrong place.
If Grapefruit ever closes unexpectedly, it offers to share a short report the next time it opens. Nothing is sent unless you choose to.
