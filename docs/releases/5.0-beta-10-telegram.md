🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 10

• Library and Podcasts: the title now scrolls away with the page, and the chips slide up to the top and stay there, floating over the page as it scrolls underneath (like the mini player and tabs at the bottom).
• Turned sideways or on a tablet, the chips float in a column at the left, and albums slide under it as you scroll; the title scrolls with them.
• Search is a chip now, with its icon and label: first in the row, before On This Device in the Library and before Discover in Podcasts (at the top of the column when sideways). It replaces the round search button by the title.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository.
