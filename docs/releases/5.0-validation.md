# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 — build 61

5.0 is 5.0 beta 11 (build 60) as stable: the same code, with versionCode 61 and versionName 5.0.
What it brings over 4.1.2 (betas 1 to 11; details in their validation notes):

- Design: Ui.PILL controls (chips, buttons, fields, notices, the mini player and tab bar), rounded
  artwork and cards (with play buttons on album, playlist and podcast cards), the floating pill tab
  bar and mini player over the page (bottom_bars, fitPage), and Now Playing's redesign (tinted
  background, artwork glow from a cached alpha mask, transport with shuffle and repeat, rounded action
  buttons). Pill press highlights on rows; rounded-square highlights on cards.
- Layouts: tab rail and two-column Now Playing on wide and square windows (Ui.wide); sideways card
  pages on wide windows (RowAdapter.landscape).
- Library and Podcasts sections (SectionMenu): chips switch sections in place (MainActivity.showLibrary,
  showPodcasts), kept while the tab is open; the title scrolls with the page and the chips float at
  the top (FloatingChips) or in a column on wide windows; Search chip; source picker.
- One round app icon and launcher entry (adaptive icon, mipmap-anydpi-v26), generated by
  tools/brand/logo.py; per-theme icons and AppIcon removed.
- Themes: Grapefruit Light pages in the grouped peach; Pitch Black in grapefruit colours on black;
  Starlight retired (shown as Grapefruit Light).
- Podcasts: no Lyrics/Karaoke/Live for episodes (Player.effectsFor turns Karaoke and Live off for
  them), 15-second skips; Podcasts tab icon (Icons.PODCASTS).
- Lyrics prefetched as songs start and saved for offline (LyricsRepository.prefetch).
- Playback load: marquees limited to three passes and only where visible; no software layer for the
  artwork shadow. Audio code (DspEngine, Dsp, VocalRemover, LiveCrowd, LiveStage) unchanged since 4.1.2.
- Discovery: generalised "How discovery exclusions work"; shorter disclosure ending "…MagicFlute LLC
  or Grapefruit."; long popups capped short of the screen's height.
- Fixes: ⋯ buttons clipped and oversized dots, playlist back button clipped, Change Artwork width.
- minSdk 17 (Android 4.2.2) as before; signed with the same key, so it updates 4.1.2 and the betas.
- Local Gradle (JDK 21): assembleDebug, assembleRelease, testDebugUnitTest, lintDebug.
