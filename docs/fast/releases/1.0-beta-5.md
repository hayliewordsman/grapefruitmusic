# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 5

- The splash screen is Music's: the TV grapefruit over the Geist Pixel Triangle wordmark ("Grapefruit" in coral, "FAST" below), as one picture in your theme's colours. It shows the moment the app starts.
- Settings › About Grapefruit FAST is now a copy of Music's About page: round back button, the icon, the wordmark on one line, version, copyright, then License, Developer and Acknowledgements.
- Full screen in landscape: on a phone, turn it sideways while watching to go full screen, and upright again to come out. Full screen turns the picture sideways. Full screen is now the picture on black.
- Fixed: on a phone, the last Settings rows (About) could stay hidden under the floating Continue watching pill and tabs.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over betas 3 and 4.

- 29 new channels, 57 in all, with region filters for Canada, the United States, the UK, Europe and Australia:
  - News: Cheddar News, BBC News, Sky News, Reuters 60, Bloomberg TV+, CBC News, DW News and ABC News Australia.
  - UK: Top Gear, Midsomer Murders, Classic Doctor Who, BBC Drama, The Great British Baking Channel, The Graham Norton Show, The Repair Shop, Come Dine With Me, BBC Earth and The Jamie Oliver Channel.
  - Canada: Heartland, Murdoch Mysteries, Mayday: Air Disaster, Property Brothers, Love Nature and GustoTV.
  - United States: MythBusters, Family Feud Classic, PBS Nature, Little House on the Prairie and Lassie.
- News is non-partisan: no right-wing or partisan channels, nothing News Corp or Fox owned, no Piers Morgan. Bias ratings are recorded for every news source.
- A channel's details say where it can be watched. DW News and ABC News Australia stream worldwide; Xumo's channels are offered in the United States.
- Search finds channels by region and country ("UK", "Australia").

All 57 channels reached media through their official sources in a full probe from the US. CBC's local news channels need a CBC Gem sign-in and are not included.
