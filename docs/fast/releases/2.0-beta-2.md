# Grapefruit TV 2.0 beta 2

- Fixed: selecting text in a dialog's field (a double-tap or long press on the prefilled name in Add a Playlist, for example) closed the app on Android 4.x. Dialogs now use Grapefruit's own dialog window, as in Music, and on Android 4.x their fields don't open the selection bar that caused it.
- A prefilled field selects its text when you tap it, so typing replaces it.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over beta 1.
