# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 4

- Settings is laid out like Music's: Appearance, Watching, Channels and About sections with accent titles, rounded rows showing what each is set to, a note under each section, and Music's grapefruit switches.
- New settings: Continue Watching (show your last channel above the tabs) and Keep Screen On (while a channel plays). Favourites shows how many you have, with Clear Favourites.
- Settings › About Grapefruit FAST: the logo and wordmark, version, licence and source code, developer and acknowledgements. Tap a link to open it, or touch and hold to copy.
- The Geist Pixel Triangle wordmark: "Grapefruit" in coral and "FAST" in the text colour, on the splash screen, the About page and the side rail.
- A splash screen: the TV grapefruit and the wordmark on your theme's colour as the app starts. On Android 12 and later it follows Android's own splash, in your theme's colour from Android 13.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key, like beta 3; installs over beta 3.

The channel catalog and playback are unchanged from beta 1; see docs/fast/validation.md. JP5S hardware playback remains untested. Provider ads and regional restrictions apply.
