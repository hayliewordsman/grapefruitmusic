# Grapefruit TV & Movies 3.0

Grapefruit TV is now **Grapefruit TV & Movies**: live TV, free films, your own IPTV, a guide and recordings, in one place. It installs over Grapefruit TV 2.0 (same app, same permanent signing key).

## New

- **Movies:** a tab of free films that are legal to watch: in the public domain in the United States, or released under Creative Commons licences by their makers. 32 films, picked by hand (nothing discriminatory): film noir, comedies, silent classics, horror, westerns and animation, from His Girl Friday and Night of the Living Dead to Sita Sings the Blues and the Blender Foundation's open movies. They stream from the Internet Archive and can be downloaded to watch offline.
- **On Demand:** when one of your sources offers films and series, an On Demand tab appears: Films, Series (seasons and episodes), Downloads and your service's own groups. Download films and episodes for offline viewing. Downloads resume after a dropped connection and are Wi-Fi only unless you change that.
- **Guide:** the Guide chip in Live TV (or Settings › Guide in Live TV) shows each channel's programmes beside it; drag sideways through the day. Programmes come from your source's guide where it publishes one. Elsewhere it shows hour-long blocks on news channels and half-hour blocks on others, labelled "Program information currently unavailable". A channel's page shows what's on now and next.
- **Recordings:** a DVR for free channels and your IPTV channels, saved on this device. Record from a programme in the guide, a channel's Record button, or Schedule a Recording. The Recordings tab has All, Finished and Scheduled. Recordings start on time and keep going with the app in the background or swiped out of Recents (a notification shows while they run), and are set again after a restart. A force stop in Android's settings cancels them until you next open the app. Encrypted streams, and streams that keep their sound separately, can't be recorded.
- **Streams at a Time:** watching, recording and downloading stay within your IPTV plan's stream limit (a login's limit is read from the service; set a playlist's under Settings › Your Sources). If a scheduled recording needs the stream you're watching, playback stops and says why.
- **Floating player** (from 2.5): leave a channel, film or recording and it keeps playing in a small window you can drag aside. Tap it to go back; ✕ stops it. One stream at a time: tapping another channel stops the one playing. Settings › Watching › Floating Player.
- Films, episodes and recordings have a seek bar, Pause, back 10 s and forward 30 s, and pick up where you left off.
- Search and Favourites take in films and series too.

## Fixed

- Chip rows keep their place when a chip is tapped, on every tab, instead of jumping back to the start.
- Your chosen filters, and a film you were watching, come back if Android restarts the app in the background.

## Removed from plans

- Background play (sound with the screen off or in another app). The in-app floating player stays.
