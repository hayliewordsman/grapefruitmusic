# Grapefruit TV 2.0

Grapefruit FAST is now Grapefruit TV: all your live TV in one place.

- Your own IPTV: add a service you subscribe to, with its M3U playlist address or its server, username and password (Settings › Your Sources). Its channels appear under the Added chip, by name. Grapefruit TV doesn't provide, sell or recommend any service; only add ones you're entitled to use.
- Your TV Apps: the streaming and TV-provider apps on your device (Crave, Citytv+, Sling, Hulu, Paramount+, Fibe TV, Ignite TV, Xfinity, DIRECTV, DISH, Shaw and more) sit at the top of Live TV and open with a tap. Add App pins any other. You sign in and watch in those apps.
- 59 free built-in channels, as in 1.0, listed wherever you are.
- The new name and Geist Pixel Triangle wordmark on the splash screen, About page, side rail and TV banner.
- A Paste button in forms, and crash reports you can choose to share (logins and addresses left out).
- Live TV drops its tagline; Settings › Channels & Availability explains built-in channels, your sources and Your TV Apps.
- Fixed on Android 4.x: selecting or pasting text in a dialog's field no longer closes the app.
- Channel names only; no programme guide.
- Android 4.2.2 and later: phones, tablets, square screens and Android TV. Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over Grapefruit FAST 1.0.
