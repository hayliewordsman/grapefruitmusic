🍊 Grapefruit Music 4.1 beta 4

Live Mode, rebuilt:
• A new stadium crowd, synthesized from scratch: thousands of modelled hand claps, hundreds of cheering voices, whistles, in a simulated stadium.
• The crowd cheers before the first note, then settles as the song gets going.
• It sings along with the lead vocal, louder in the choruses, and cheers when the song lifts.
• The ovation starts as the song ends: when a fade-out starts or the music stops, not after a silent tail.
• The song rings in a stadium instead of the old echoes.
• Still original GPL audio: no recordings, no sound libraries, no trained crowd model.

Fixed:
• The scrubber landing away from where you let go, with an effect on (Karaoke, Live, equalizer, boost or mono).
• Podcast times running past the end and stopping at -0:00, including downloaded episodes.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository. Please try Live Mode on the JP5S and say how it sounds, and whether anything stutters.
