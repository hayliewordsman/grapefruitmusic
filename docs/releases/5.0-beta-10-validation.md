# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 10 — build 59

- SectionMenu builds each section's page instead of pinning a menu above (or beside) it:
  - Up and down (ListView pages: Recently Added, Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs; Discover,
    Subscriptions, Downloads): the title is the list's first header and scrolls with it; the next
    header is an empty slot the chip row's height. The chip row floats over the list in a
    FrameLayout, placed (each frame, in an OnPreDrawListener) where the slot is, or at the top once
    the slot has scrolled past. There it sits on Ui.fadingFill: the page colour, fading to clear
    over its last 20dp, faded in over the first 12dp of scrolling, so the page passes under it as
    under the mini player and tabs. Notes (Discover's disclosure, Downloads' hint) and Shuffle
    All / New Playlist follow as headers and scroll with the page.
  - Wide screens: the chips are a 232dp column floating at the left (fading at its right edge);
    lists and card grids are padded by its width, the title scrolls with them (the grid's title
    slot for cards), and cards scroll under the column. The card height reserve includes the title.
  - Podcasts offline (not a list): title and chips pinned above the message, or beside it.
- Search is a chip (icon and "Search"), first in the row or column: before On This Device in the
  Library (Search tab) and before Discover in Podcasts (Search Podcasts). The round search button
  by the title is gone. A hairline (a gap, stacked) separates Search and the source from the
  sections. At the first section the row starts at its beginning, so Search shows.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): Recently Added at rest, part-way
  and scrolled (chips floating), Songs at rest and scrolled, and Discover, in Grapefruit Dark and
  Light (360x760dp), Pitch Black on a landscape phone (760x360dp, cards scrolled under the column)
  and Grapefruit Light on a tablet (1024x600dp).
- Version 5.0-beta-10 (59): a prerelease, not published to the F-Droid repository.
- Local Gradle (JDK 21): assembleDebug, assembleRelease, testDebugUnitTest, lintDebug.
