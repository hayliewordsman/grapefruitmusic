# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 3 — build 52

## One icon, one launcher entry

- The icon: the Telegram channel's grapefruit, round, as on the splash screen, the same in every
  theme. tools/brand/logo.py draws it with the logos: mipmap-*/ic_launcher.png (48dp, round, for
  Android 4.2 to 7.1), mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml (adaptive: the picture filling a 108dp
  foreground layer, laid out for the 72dp launchers show, on its own coral; masked round on most
  launchers) and drawable-*/ic_about.png (88dp, the About page).
- The launcher entry is MainActivity itself. Until now there were four activity-aliases, one per
  theme with its own icon, switched by AppIcon. Those are gone (with AppIcon, the per-theme
  pictures, tools/icons/make_icons.py and the note in Settings). MainActivity was never switched
  off, so after the update no one can be left without an entry; the cost is that a launcher may
  drop the old home-screen shortcut (it pointed to an alias) once.

## The bars float, on every Android version

- activity_main: the page fills its column, and the mini player and tabs sit in a group over its
  bottom, on a fade from the page's colour (taps on the fade go through to the page). Before, they
  were stacked under the page, so nothing scrolled behind them.
- MainActivity.fitPage (from BaseFragment.onViewCreated, and again when the group's height
  changes: the mini player comes and goes, the screen turns) gives every page's scrolling views
  (lists, scroll views, the sideways grid) bottom padding the group's height, without clipping, so
  the page scrolls out from under the bars and its end stops above them. Notices sit above the
  group. On wide screens (rail) only the mini player floats.

## Rounder

- Settings rows are pills (28dp ends); Now Playing's action tiles are fully round.
