🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 4.1 beta 6

• New name: Grapefruit Music & Podcasts (just "Grapefruit" under its icon).
• New logo, lettered in Geist Pixel, and a splash screen in your theme's colours.
• The whole app now uses the Geist typeface.
• Podcasts: the time no longer reaches 0:00 minutes before an episode ends. Episodes with ads added as they're served now show their real length, streamed or downloaded.
• Still runs on Android 4.2.2 and later.
