# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.1 beta 1 — build 62

- PodcastsFragment: on a wide screen (SectionMenu.wide), Discover and Subscriptions build a
  HorizontalGrid, as the Library's card sections do: RowAdapter.setStacked with landscapeRows
  (SectionMenu.WIDE_RESERVED_DP, plus room for Discover's note), padded by the chip column's
  width and laid out by SectionMenu.page(grid, status), so the title, the note and the status
  scroll with the first columns and the cards scroll under the floating column. Before, they were
  a ListView of cards two across, scrolling down. Downloads stays a list (as Songs and Playlists
  are); show and search pages are unchanged; portrait is unchanged.
- The status line (Loading, Nothing here yet, errors) is hidden when it has nothing to say
  (showStatus), so it leaves no gap under the title.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): Library, Discover (at the start and
  scrolled), Subscriptions and Downloads on a landscape phone (760x360dp, Grapefruit Dark), a tablet
  (1024x600dp, Grapefruit Light) and a portrait phone (360x760dp, Pitch Black).
- Version 5.1-beta-1 (62): a prerelease, not published to the F-Droid repository.
- Local Gradle (JDK 21): assembleDebug, assembleRelease, testDebugUnitTest, lintDebug.
