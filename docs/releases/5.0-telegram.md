🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0

A new look, top to bottom:
• Pills everywhere: buttons, chips, text fields, search boxes and notices are rounded pills, and artwork and cards are rounded too, with a play button right on album and playlist cards.
• The tab bar and mini player float over the page as pills (the mini player with round artwork), and the page scrolls underneath them.
• Now Playing is redesigned: artwork with a soft glow in its own colour, a background tinted to match, a big round play button with shuffle and repeat beside it, and rounded buttons for Lyrics, Karaoke, Live and Up Next.
• Landscape and square screens (Unihertz Titan, Clicks Communicator, tablets) get a side tab rail and a two-column Now Playing.
• Library and Podcasts: chips for each section (Recently Added, Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs; Discover, Subscriptions, Downloads) switch the page in place and stay put; the title scrolls away and the chips float at the top. Search is a chip, and On This Device picks where the music comes from. Sideways, the chips float in a column at the left.
• One round app icon (the Telegram channel's grapefruit) in every theme, with a single launcher entry.
• Themes: Grapefruit Light's pages share Settings' peach, and Pitch Black stays black with Grapefruit's coral and cream. Starlight is retired; it becomes Grapefruit Light.
• The Podcasts tab has its own icon.

Podcasts:
• Episodes drop the buttons that don't apply (Lyrics, Karaoke, Live), and previous/next jump 15 seconds back and forward.

Lyrics:
• A song's lyrics are looked up a few seconds after it starts (your server or a .lrc file first, then LRCLIB) and saved, so they're ready when you open Lyrics and work offline.

Also:
• Less work for older tablets while music plays, to give Karaoke Mode and Live Mode room: long titles scroll a few times and only where you can see them, and Now Playing's artwork glow is drawn by the graphics chip.
• Podcasts' Exclusions & sources explains the discovery policy as a whole, and long popups leave room at the top and bottom of the screen.
• Fixed: the ⋯ button beside songs cut off on playlists and albums, the back button cut off at the top of playlist pages, and the tag editor's Change Artwork button stretching across the screen.
• Still runs on Android 4.2.2 and later; updates over 4.1.2 with the same signing key.
