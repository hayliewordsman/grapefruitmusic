# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 4.1.2 — build 49

A fix release: podcasts stuttered on the JP5S (Android 4.2) with the screen locked, downloaded
episodes too, with the equalizer or Volume Boost on (Grapefruit's own engine). Songs didn't.

## Cause

The one thing that runs only for podcasts while they play is the checkpoint of where you are, every
15 seconds. It went into the app's settings (SharedPreferences): every checkpoint rewrote the whole
settings file and forced it to disk (fsync, through QueuedWork). With the screen off, Android 4.x
kernels hold back writing to storage, so each forced write had a backlog to flush, and reading the
episode (and the decoder that needs it) waited behind it; Grapefruit's engine keeps half a second
of sound ahead, which wasn't always enough. With the screen on, writes go out as they come, and
songs have no checkpoint, which is why neither stuttered. Not reproduced on the device here (none);
the fix removes the forced writes during playback either way.

## Fix

- PodcastPositions: positions have a small file of their own (podcast-positions.txt, "id TAB ms"
  lines, the newest 500), written on a low-priority thread by writing a new copy and renaming it,
  with no fsync; an unchanged position (paused) isn't written at all. Positions saved by 4.1.1 and
  earlier move over from the settings on first use, once.
- The one-time frame scan of a downloaded episode's real length (4.1) runs at background priority,
  so reading the file never competes with playback.
