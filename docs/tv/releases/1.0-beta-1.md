# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 1

The first Grapefruit live-TV app, installed alongside Music & Podcasts.

- Grapefruit Dark, Light and Pitch Black themes, Geist typography and a TV-shaped Grapefruit icon.
- Channel browsing with country/genre filters, favourites, city/channel search and last-channel access.
- Adaptive HLS video with Auto, Data saver and Compatibility modes; full screen, remote controls and bounded reconnects.
- Initial US/Canada channel catalog; CP24 excluded because no stream was obtained from cp24.com.
- Android 4.2.2/API 17 minimum, including the intended jailbroken JP5S, subject to device decoder and stream availability testing.

This is a test beta. All 28 refreshed channels passed official-source playlist/rendition/media checks across the full run and a recorded follow-up. Global National and Winnipeg had intermittent runtime proxy denials; no persistent channel failure remained in the follow-up. H.264/AAC was identified in bounded samples for every channel. JP5S hardware playback and bandwidth adaptation under throttled Wi-Fi remain untested. Global video requires Auto under the preserved quality caps. See docs/fast/validation.md for the full evidence and limitations. There is no programme EPG, recording, time shifting or subscription/DRM integration. Provider ads and regional restrictions apply.
