Grapefruit Audio (until 6.0, Grapefruit Music & Podcasts) is a small, native music, podcast and audiobook player for Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean) and later. It plays music you own: files on your device, and your own Navidrome, Subsonic or Jellyfin servers.

• Download albums, playlists and songs for offline listening
• Discover podcasts, subscribe, download episodes and receive periodic new-episode notifications; import and export your subscriptions as OPML
• Audiobooks: DRM-free M4B and MP3 books (Libro.fm's downloads included) with chapters, and your place kept
• Playback speed from 0.8× to 2× with the pitch kept, set separately for music, podcasts and audiobooks
• A sleep timer for music, podcasts, audiobooks and radio
• Search music, radio stations and podcasts together
• Time-synced lyrics from .lrc files, your server or LRCLIB, with a timing nudge for lyrics that run early or late
• Internet radio from the Radio Browser directory, and FM radio on tablets whose built-in radio Grapefruit supports
• Volume Match, Volume Boost, a 10-band equalizer, Mono Audio and Karaoke Mode
• Live Mode: any song as a stadium show, with a crowd that cheers, sings along and applauds
• Edit song tags and artwork
• Three themes

Grapefruit draws its own controls, so it looks the same on every Android version, and brings its own modern TLS (Conscrypt), so it can still reach today's HTTPS servers on Android 4.2.
