Grapefruit Maps 1.0 beta 2 updates Victory Break and adds universal Grapefruit theming.

- Victory Blue's five-tap Developer / Haylie Easter egg now shows a side-profile Trump caricature with an orange tan, pout, wispy blond combover and red cap. Tap the head to send poop emojis out of his mouth and onto the map. Back clears the drops.
- The head is a bundled PNG, and the map and poop are locally drawn shapes. No system emoji support, image service or network connection is needed to play. The editable artwork and packaging recipe are included in source.
- Maps now reads the same optional, read-only theme contract as the JP5S Grapefruit Settings build. Dark, Light, Pitch Black and Victory Blue apply when Maps resumes after a new universal selection. Local choices last until the next universal selection; no installed Settings companion leaves the local theme unchanged.
- Maps uses the general four-theme selection, including Victory Blue, rather than the three-theme media fallback. Switching away from Blue closes the game and clears its drops.

The Maps icon, centered Geist Pixel Triangle About wordmark and consistent drawn back buttons are retained. Android 4.2.2 support is retained, with the same application ID and signing certificate for an in-place update. Actual JP5S hardware remains untested here.
