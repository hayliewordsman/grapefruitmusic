Grapefruit Music is now free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later, and has a new app ID (io.github.hayliewordsman.grapefruitmusic) so it can join F-Droid. Android sees it as a new app: install it beside the old Grapefruit, sign in to your servers again and re-download offline music, then uninstall the old one. Settings › About shows the licence and links to the source code.
