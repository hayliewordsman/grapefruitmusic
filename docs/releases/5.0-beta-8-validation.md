# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 8 — build 57

- AlbumFragment (playlists and albums): the round back button moves from the page header into
  the top bar beside Edit, and it kept the header's 6dp bottom margin. Centred vertically in the
  bar, that margin placed it 3dp above the bar's top, where it was clipped. It now goes into the
  bar with its own 40dp size and no margins.
- Icons.PODCASTS (new): a solid head (r 2.7) on a rounded stem, inside two 1.9-unit rings open at
  the bottom, with round ends, on the 24-unit grid like the other tab icons. It replaces
  Icons.HEADPHONES on the Podcasts tab (bottom pill and wide-screen rail). Headphones stay on
  the FM radio "plug in headphones" tip.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): the tab icons at 96, 24 and 22dp
  on light and dark; the tab bar in Grapefruit Dark and Starlight; the wide-screen rail in Pitch
  Black; the top of a playlist and an album page.
