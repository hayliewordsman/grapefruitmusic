# Active discovery editorial policy

## How discovery exclusions work

The main Podcasts discovery list excludes Turning Point USA and The Daily Wire publications, including archived network publications, plus verified personal shows of their founders or executive leadership. This is a disclosed publisher/leadership editorial decision, not a claim that every show, episode, leader or guest has engaged in hate or deception. Current and former founders/executives are included where the show identity is verified. Ordinary former employees, guests, unrelated namesakes and independently published shows of former presenters are not automatically included.

## Limits, corrections and access

Review snapshot: 30 October 2026. Misinformation means demonstrably false or materially misleading factual claims; disinformation requires evidence of knowing deception. We do not infer intent solely from false-claim ratings. Political affiliation, religion and family relationships alone are not conduct findings. Publisher-wide editorial decisions extend beyond the individual examples examined. Source age and corrections are disclosed here. Exact show/feed identifiers and verified publisher names are used; no title keywords or inferred listener politics are used. This is a maintained snapshot, not a guarantee that every future renamed, transferred or newly created leadership show is identified. The network publisher rule also covers new shows bearing the same directory publisher identity. Unresolved RSS identities now use the explicitly approved exact-title fallback documented below; unrelated search results are not excluded. Search results, direct RSS access, subscriptions, Recently Added, downloads and playback are unaffected. The directory itself may not list every show. Future corrections, ownership changes and feed migrations require review.



The exact active feed registry is [podcast-discovery-feeds.csv](podcast-discovery-feeds.csv). The in-app explanation is [the bundled policy](../app/src/main/assets/podcast-discovery-policy.html).

## Feed ingestion and validation

RSS/Atom XML now streams from the network into a SAX parser instead of buffering a full XML document. It retains at most the first 1,000 entries and caps individual text fields at 4,096 characters, nesting at 64 levels and input at 64 MiB. The input limit is a network/resource safety budget, not an audio-download limit or an editorial criterion. DTDs and external entities are rejected; redirects retain the original feed identity and resolve media against the final address. Very large individual XML attributes remain subject to the XML parser's own allocation behavior and the input budget.

Validation: 120 unit tests, debug/release builds and Android lint; actual Charlie Kirk (17,039,593 bytes), Matt Walsh (12,791,098 bytes) and Morning Wire (9,144,369 bytes) archives each parsed to the bounded 1,000-episode window under a 48 MiB JVM heap. This is parser validation, not an Android 4.2.2 device smoke test. API 17 remains the minimum supported version.
