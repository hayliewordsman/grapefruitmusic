## Expanded criteria

- **Targeted hate/harassment:** specific documented advocacy of supremacist or extremist violence, targeted dehumanization, or targeted harassment. Attribute allegations, institutional findings and court outcomes accurately. Policy disagreement alone does not establish harassment.
- **Misinformation:** demonstrably false or materially misleading factual claims, assessed against the available evidence when published. Prefer a documented pattern: at least two distinct material examples, with transparent sourcing and preferably more than one independent checking organization.
- **Disinformation:** reserve this label for evidence of knowing deception or deceptive coordination. A false-claim rating alone does not prove intent. Court language must be confined to what the court actually addressed.
- **Corrections and age:** record corrections, retractions, unresolved disputes, source dates and changed ownership. One promptly corrected error should not create a permanent publisher penalty. Historical examples are not proof that all current episodes are false.
- **Scope:** show-specific evidence is preferred. A publisher-wide adjustment is a separate editorial choice extending beyond the individual items examined; make that limitation explicit. Do not classify all guests, employees, affiliates, co-founders or family members by association.
- **Application:** discovery recommendations only. Search, subscriptions, playback and downloads remain available. No inference of users’ political beliefs from subscription history.
