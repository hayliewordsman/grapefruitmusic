Grapefruit TV brings your live TV together in one small, native app for Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean) and later, from the makers of Grapefruit Music & Podcasts. It was called Grapefruit FAST until version 2.0.

• 59 free built-in channels, no account needed: news from Canada, the United States, the UK, Europe and Australia, and classic series, reality and lifestyle channels
• Your own IPTV: add a service you subscribe to with its M3U playlist address, or its server, username and password. Channel names only, no programme guide
• Your TV Apps: the streaming and TV-provider apps on your device sit at the top of Live TV and open with a tap
• Captions where a channel provides them
• Favourites, search by name, city or genre, and Continue watching
• Full screen, with sideways-to-full-screen on phones
• Adaptive quality, with Data saver and Compatibility modes
• Grapefruit Dark, Grapefruit Light and Pitch Black themes, in Grapefruit's floating pill design
• Phones, tablets, square screens and Android TV, with remote control support

Built-in channels come straight from broadcasters and their official distributors. Some are offered only in certain countries; if a channel's provider doesn't offer it where you are, the player says so. Their own advertisements play as usual. Grapefruit TV adds no ads of its own and no tracking.

Grapefruit TV doesn't provide, sell or recommend any IPTV service: only add ones you're entitled to use. It never signs in to your TV apps; you watch and sign in there.
