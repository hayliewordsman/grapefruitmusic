🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 4.1

• New name: Grapefruit Music & Podcasts ("Grapefruit" under its icon), with a new logo lettered in Geist Pixel, a splash screen in your theme's colours, and the Geist typeface throughout.
• Live Mode: any song as a stadium show. A synthesized crowd cheers before the first note, sings along with the lead vocal, cheers when the song lifts, and gives an ovation as it ends (waiting for the last word). Strength from 0 to 100%. Original GPL audio, no recordings.
• Now Playing: labelled action buttons (Lyrics, Karaoke, Live, Shuffle, Repeat, Up Next); long titles scroll, there and in the mini player.
• About: the name in the Geist Pixel wordmark.
• Fixed: the scrubber landing away from where you let go (with an effect on), and podcast times running out minutes before an episode ends (episodes with ads added as they're served now show their real length).
• Still runs on Android 4.2.2 and later; updates over 4.0.1 with the same signing key.
