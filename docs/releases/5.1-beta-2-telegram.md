🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.1 beta 2

• Lyrics sideways: with the phone on its side (or on a tablet or a square phone), Lyrics now fill the whole left side where the artwork was, and the song's title and artist stay on the right, above the progress bar. Up Next does the same.
• Square phones: on a narrow screen like a Unihertz Titan or a Clicks Communicator, the lyrics are sized to the column so a line holds a phrase, and Up Next leaves more room for song titles.
• The playing notification: on Android 4.2 it could show only "Grapefruit" with no song. Its pictures are now less than half the size they were (Android drops a notification that's too big without saying so), and the first notification already names the song, so it never stands alone.
• Adding a whole album to a playlist could close Grapefruit. Playlists on this device are now saved so that a fault in the phone's media library can't take Grapefruit with it; if something goes wrong you'll see a message instead. A crash now always leaves a report to share, even when the phone runs out of memory.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository.
