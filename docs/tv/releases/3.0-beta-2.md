# Grapefruit TV & Movies 3.0 beta 2

- Fixed: tapping a chip you'd scrolled to (Adventure in Movies, say) made the row jump back to the start, so you couldn't see which one was chosen. Chip rows now stay where they were, on every tab: Live TV, Movies, On Demand, Recordings, Favourites, Search, and a series' seasons. Each tab's row keeps its place when you come back to it, and a chosen chip far along the row starts in view.
- Your chosen Movies genre, On Demand chips and Recordings filter are kept if Android restarts the app in the background, and a film you were watching comes back too.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over 3.0 beta 1.
