# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 7 — build 56

- Discovery policy (assets/podcast-discovery-policy.html and docs/podcast-discovery-policy.md):
  the "How discovery exclusions work" paragraph, written when only Turning Point USA and The Daily
  Wire were listed, now describes the rules generically: network-wide publisher exclusions (with
  archived shows and new shows under the same publisher), verified personal shows of current or
  former founders and executives, and individual shows excluded for documented hate or violent
  rhetoric or documented false or misleading claims; matching by exact feed, directory listing and
  publisher name, or exact full title where a feed is unverified, never keywords, topics, guests
  or politics; not a claim about every show, episode, host or guest; Search, RSS, subscriptions,
  downloads and playback unaffected. The sections below it, the feed registry and
  DiscoveryPolicy's rules are unchanged. The in-app page drops its own heading, which repeated the
  popup's title.
- GrapefruitDialog: a centred card is at most the screen's height less a margin above and below
  (12% of the height each, at least 24dp); a long message, form or page scrolls inside it (it
  was already the shrinking part). Exclusions & sources' text no longer adds 20dp side padding
  inside the card's own.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): Exclusions & sources, Discovery
  recommendations and a short alert over Podcasts on a 360x760dp phone (card 578dp of 760),
  a 320x533dp phone and a landscape 760x360dp phone; the short alert keeps its size.
- Version 5.0-beta-7 (56): a prerelease, not published to the F-Droid repository.
- Local Gradle (JDK 21): assembleDebug, assembleRelease, testDebugUnitTest, lintDebug.
