# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 2

A little more like Music: everything is a pill, and it floats.

- The tabs float on a pill over the page: along the bottom, or down the side beside the wordmark when there's room across. Lists scroll out from under them.
- Continue watching floats above the tabs like Music's mini player, with the channel's logo and a play button.
- Channel rows are pills with round logos. The channel that's playing shows a LIVE pill.
- Genres and countries share one chip row that scrolls edge to edge; tap a country again to see both.
- The watch page has round back and favourite buttons, a picture with rounded corners, a LIVE pill, and action pills that scroll edge to edge. In full screen, tap the picture for the controls.
- Settings rows are pills showing what each is set to. Theme and quality pickers mark the current choice.
- Fixed: the outline on a pressed or selected channel sat too low, around the gap under the row rather than the row.
- Fixed: short popups no longer stretch to full height; long ones scroll with their buttons kept in sight.
- Fixed: Favourites and Search explain an empty list instead of "Try another filter".
- Fixed: the window, status bar and navigation bar take the theme's colour (no dark flash on Grapefruit Light).
- Smoother scrolling on older devices: channel rows are reused as you scroll.

The channel catalog and playback are unchanged from beta 1; see docs/fast/validation.md. JP5S hardware playback remains untested. Provider ads and regional restrictions apply.
