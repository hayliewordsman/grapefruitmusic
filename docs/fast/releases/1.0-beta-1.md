# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 1

The first Grapefruit live-TV app, installed alongside Music & Podcasts.

- Grapefruit Dark, Light and Pitch Black themes, Geist typography and a TV-shaped Grapefruit icon.
- Channel browsing with country/genre filters, favourites, city/channel search and last-channel access.
- Adaptive HLS video with Auto, Data saver and Compatibility modes; full screen, remote controls and bounded reconnects.
- Initial US/Canada channel catalog; CP24 excluded because no stream was obtained from cp24.com.
- Android 4.2.2/API 17 minimum, including the intended jailbroken JP5S, subject to device decoder and stream availability testing.

This is an initial test beta. Channel candidates have not been verified end-to-end from this restricted environment. There is no programme EPG, recording, time shifting or subscription/DRM integration. Provider ads and regional restrictions apply.
