# Grapefruit TV: requests for official distribution access

Regional FAST services (Pluto TV, Plex, Samsung TV Plus, The Roku Channel, LG Channels, Xumo) license their channels per country and offer them to third-party apps only by agreement. Grapefruit TV does not impersonate their own apps or get around sign-in, bot checks or geoblocks. These are drafts for asking each one for official access. Adjust before sending.

Where to send:

- Pluto TV (Paramount): partnerships through paramount.com's business contact pages; Pluto's channel partners page (corporate.pluto.tv).
- Plex: plex.tv/about (business and partnerships contact).
- Samsung TV Plus: samsungtvplus.com, partner enquiries.
- Xumo (Comcast/Charter): xumo.com, business enquiries (already the US source for many channels).
- The Roku Channel: Roku's content partner enquiries (developer.roku.com, business).
- LG Channels: LG Ad Solutions partner contact.

## Draft

Subject: Distribution request: Grapefruit TV, a free Android TV and phone app (Canada, UK, Australia)

Hello,

I maintain Grapefruit TV, a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) Android app for live free ad-supported TV. It runs on Android 4.2 to 16 phones, tablets and TVs, requires no account and adds no tracking or advertising of its own. Provider ads are kept intact.

I'd like to offer your free channels to viewers in Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia through an official integration, rather than unofficial access. Could you tell me whether you have a distribution or developer programme for third-party apps, and what it requires (for example a partner key, ad measurement, branding or reporting)?

The app's source is at github.com/hayliewordsman/grapefruitmusic (tv/). I'm happy to follow your technical and brand requirements, and to identify the app honestly in every request.
