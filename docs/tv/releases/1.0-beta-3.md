# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 3

- The tab bar has icons, like Music: a television for Live TV, a star for Favourites, Search and Settings. The chosen tab shows its name on the accent pill; the bar is only as wide as its tabs. Down the side, every tab shows its name.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key, the same as Music. From now on every FAST build, beta and stable, installs over the one before. Beta 1 and 2 test builds used a debug key: uninstall them once first.
- Android 15 and 16 (Pixel 10 and the like): the page stays clear of the status bar, navigation bar, camera cutout and keyboard; full screen hides the bars (swipe to peek); Back steps out of full screen, the watch page and tabs instead of closing the app; status and navigation bar icons follow the theme.
- Fixed a crash at launch on Android 11 and later.
- Android 4.2.2 remains the minimum.

The channel catalog and playback are unchanged from beta 1; see docs/fast/validation.md. JP5S hardware playback remains untested. Provider ads and regional restrictions apply.
