🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 6

• The mini player is a pill now, with round artwork, like the tab bar under it.
• More pills: text fields and search boxes, Radio's genre buttons, notices at the bottom of the screen, and the highlight when you press a song, album or station row.
• The ⋯ button beside a song isn't cut off on playlists and albums any more, and its dots are sized to fit the circle everywhere.
• Change Artwork in the tag editor fits its words instead of stretching across the screen, and Cancel and Save sit clear of the screen's edges.
• Tidier corners: the equalizer's curve sits on a rounded card, a playlist cover's Edit label is a pill, and station and tag editor artwork is rounded like the rest.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository.
