# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 11

- Square screens, like the Clicks Communicator: FAST uses its portrait layout, with the tabs along the bottom, instead of the side rail. The side rail is now only for screens that are clearly landscape.
- On short screens, Continue watching sits at the top of the channel list and scrolls away, so more channels fit. The watch page keeps the picture to 40% of the height, hides the tabs (the round back button is there) and leaves room for the channel list below.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over betas 3 to 10.
