🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 9

• Grapefruit Light: every page now has the same peach background as Settings, instead of the paler cream.
• Starlight is retired. If you were using it, you'll get Grapefruit Light, its nearest match.
• Pitch Black is more grapefruit: still black, now with the coral accent, cream text and warmer controls.
• Karaoke Mode and Live Mode on older tablets: their sound code is unchanged since 4.1.2, but the new look was asking more of the processor while music played. Now long titles scroll three times per song and only where you can see them (the mini player's title rests while Now Playing is open), and the artwork's shadow is no longer redrawn on the processor. Please tell us if Karaoke and Live still stutter.
