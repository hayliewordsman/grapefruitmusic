# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 9

- Captions, where a channel provides them: a Captions pill on the watch page (and in full screen) turns them on and off. It appears only when the stream has captions. Nearly every channel does: closed captions in the video, or subtitle tracks (DW News, ABC News Australia).
- Captions show white on a dark backing near the bottom of the picture, in your language where a stream offers a choice.
- Settings › Watching › Captions remembers your choice. Until you choose, FAST follows Android's own caption setting and text size (Android 4.4 and later).
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over betas 3 to 8.
