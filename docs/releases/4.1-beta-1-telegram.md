🍊 Grapefruit Music 4.1 beta 1

• New Live Mode: a song-derived crowd singalong, procedural cheering/applause and venue ambience.
• Its own 0–100% strength slider, alongside Karaoke Mode.
• Six labeled Now Playing actions, with Live between Karaoke and Shuffle.
• More space between the volume slider and action row.
• Original procedural crowd-model training recipe and FOSS provenance included.
• Android 4.2.2+ support and the existing signing identity retained.

This beta uses a stylized crowd simulation. Listening quality and performance on older devices need testing. Available through the GitHub Actions beta artifact archive; no F-Droid update.
