# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 6

- CNN Headlines joins the news channels.
- Cheddar News now plays from Cheddar's own live stream (cheddar.com), so it isn't limited to Xumo's US copy. Xumo stays as a fallback.
- Settings › Channels & Availability no longer carries editorial notes about news selection.
- 58 channels. Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over betas 3 to 5.

Beta 6 looks for broadcasters' own public live streams in place of Xumo's US-only copies. Sky News, Bloomberg and Gusto TV turn automated browsers away, Euronews is unavailable in the US, BBC News and Australian commercial news need a sign-in, so those stay as they are or remain out.
