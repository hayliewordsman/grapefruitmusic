# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 1 — build 50

The 5.0 look, adapted from a music-player design the developer chose (one screenshot: a dark home
screen with chips and cards, and a tinted Now Playing), without its Liquid Glass and in Grapefruit's
own features, themes, colours, Geist type and toggles. Android 4.2.2 and up, as before.

## What changed

- Theme tokens (attrs, all four themes): gfAccentEnd (the accent's gradient), gfOnAccent (text and
  icons on it: white on the light themes, near-black on the dark ones, for contrast) and gfOutline
  (hairlines). Starlight's and Pitch Black's greys moved away from iOS's.
- Ui: chips and chip rows, round buttons (styleCircle), pill buttons (the main one in the accent
  gradient), page headers with a round back button and round actions by a big title, section
  titles, the "⋯" as a small round button, rounded artwork by default. All GradientDrawables and
  Grapefruit's own views (no elevation, clipPath or vector drawables: Android 4.2-safe).
- MainActivity: the tabs are a floating pill (the chosen tab on the accent gradient, with its
  name when there's 360dp across); the mini player is a rounded card with the artist; both get
  shorter on short screens.
- Library: a search button by the title; the source and Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs as
  chips. Album, playlist and podcast cards have a play button (plays from the start; a show from
  its newest episode). Rows: rounded artwork, no arrows, no lines between them.
- Podcasts: a search button by the title; Subscriptions and Downloads as chips.
- Now Playing: the background is a gradient from the artwork's colour (NowPlayingCard.colourOf,
  stronger on dark themes) instead of a blurred copy (Blur removed); round close and "⋯" buttons
  with the album, show or "FM Radio" between them; rounded artwork with a glow in its colour, which
  no longer shrinks when paused; the title, then "artist · n/total"; a thicker scrubber with a
  ringed thumb; elapsed and total time (not time left); shuffle, back, play (accent gradient, in a
  glow), on and repeat in one row (smaller on narrow or short screens).
- Podcasts in Now Playing: back and on are 15 seconds (Player.skipBy; new BACK_15/FORWARD_15
  icons, drawn in physical pixels); Lyrics, Karaoke and Live are hidden; and Player.effectsFor
  leaves Karaoke Mode and Live Mode off for podcast episodes.
- Settings: sentence-case section titles in the accent, roomier rounded rows, no arrows.
- Segmented controls are chips; dialogs' buttons are pills (the main one in the accent gradient);
  menus are one rounded sheet with a handle, the title on the left and Cancel inside.
