# Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 2 — build 51

The 5.0 look on landscape and square screens.

- Ui.wide(config): width at least 0.85 of the height, a phone on its side, a tablet in landscape, or
  a square screen (Unihertz Titan, Titan Pocket, Clicks Communicator).
- MainActivity.fitBars: on a wide screen the tab pill moves into a rail at the left of the page
  column (activity_main: a frame and a column; the tab bar moves between them as the screen turns,
  without restarting the activity), icons only; the mini player stays at the bottom of the column.
- RowAdapter.landscape (Library and Browse scroll sideways): only when the screen is wide (more than
  1.2 times as wide as tall), not just sideways, so square screens keep the up-and-down pages. Their
  sideways rows no longer count the tabs' height.
- Now Playing: two columns whenever Ui.wide (was: landscape only), the artwork 44%, and the title,
  scrubber, transport, volume and the buttons row (moved into the column) at the right. The transport
  takes the biggest of five sizes that fits across its column (from 42/54/80dp down to 28/34/46dp).
- Grouped pages (Settings and its pages): at most 640dp wide, centred.
