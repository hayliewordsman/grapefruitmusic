🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 1

A new look, built on the same features, themes, colours and Geist type, and still running on Android 4.2.2:
• Tabs: a floating pill; the tab you're on shows its name.
• Pages: big titles with round buttons beside them (search, back), and chips for where your music comes from and for Playlists, Artists, Albums and Songs.
• Album cards: rounded artwork with a play button to start the whole album, playlist or show straight from Library.
• The mini player is a card above the tabs, with the artist under the title.
• Now Playing: a background tinted by the artwork, round buttons at the top with the album (or show) between them, the title with "artist · 3/12", elapsed and total time, and shuffle, back, a glowing play button, on and repeat in one row.
• Settings: rounded rows without arrows. Menus come up as one rounded sheet, and choices like Headphones/Speaker are chips.
• Grapefruit's toggles stay as they are.

Podcasts:
• In Now Playing, back and on skip 15 seconds back and forward.
• Lyrics, Karaoke and Live are gone from podcast episodes, and Karaoke Mode and Live Mode no longer apply to them (Volume Boost, the equalizer and Mono Audio still do).

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository.
