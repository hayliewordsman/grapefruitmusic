# Grapefruit FAST 1.0 beta 7

- The header scrolls as in Music: the title scrolls away with the channels, and the chips (and Search's field) stay at the top once they get there, floating over the list as it fades under them.
- Region: FAST works out where you are from the phone itself (its mobile network or SIM country, then its region setting). Nothing is sent anywhere and no permission is needed. Outside the United States, channels only offered there (Xumo's) are hidden; Settings › Region lets you choose.
- Signed with the permanent Grapefruit key; installs over betas 3 to 6.
