# Grapefruit Audio 6.0 beta 2 — build 66

## The wordmark's triangles

- Reported: the wordmark didn't look like Geist Pixel's Triangle shape, unlike Grapefruit TV &
  Movies'. It was the Triangle font, too small to show it: Geist Pixel draws a letter from 38/1000-em
  elements, 19 to a capital, so an element is 0.038 of the text size. Fitted to the 280dp splash
  picture, beta 1's "Grapefruit Audio" (16 characters) had 1.32dp triangles and its tagline (31)
  0.63dp; the TV app's splash, with "Grapefruit" (10) across the same width, has 2.17dp and 1.74dp
  (5.1's splash had 2.10dp). The About page: 1.14dp and 0.65dp. At 1-3 screen pixels a triangle
  reads as a dot.
- Two lines as before ("Grapefruit Audio", then "Music, Podcasts, and Audiobooks"), made as large as
  the screen allows (the other layouts considered, wrapping the tagline or stacking "Grapefruit" over
  "Audio", were set aside):
  - tools/brand/logo.py: the tagline is sized to the name's width (it was narrower), and the splash
    picture comes at three widths, by Android's available-width qualifiers: 304dp (drawable-*dpi,
    8dp clear each side of a 320dp phone), 336dp (drawable-w360dp-*dpi) and 368dp
    (drawable-w400dp-*dpi). A window background's picture isn't scaled, so it can't be wider than
    the narrowest screen it's used on. Triangles (name/tagline): 1.45/0.74dp from 320dp,
    1.60/0.82dp from 360dp, 1.75/0.90dp from 400dp (beta 1: 1.32/0.63dp everywhere).
  - The About page fits each line to the page's width (up to 44sp and 23sp, on a tablet): on a 360dp
    phone about 41sp and 21sp, 1.55dp and 0.79dp triangles (was 1.14dp and 0.65dp); on a 320dp
    one 1.36dp and 0.70dp.
  - Hinting: the wordmark font is now written without TrueType hinting (fontTools subset
    hinting off: no fpgm, prep or cvt), and the About page draws it with Paint.HINTING_OFF and
    subpixel positioning (API 14+), so nothing snaps the tiny triangles to the pixel grid.
- Rendered with a temporary Robolectric test (not committed): the splash screen and About page on a
  320dp hdpi phone, a 360dp xhdpi phone, a 411dp xxhdpi phone and a phone on its side; each picked
  its own splash width, none cut off, and zoomed in, the triangles show as triangles.
