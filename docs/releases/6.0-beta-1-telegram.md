🍊🎧 Grapefruit Audio 6.0 beta 1

Grapefruit Music & Podcasts is now Grapefruit Audio: Music, Podcasts, and Audiobooks. 5.2 was skipped: its features are here.

• Audiobooks: a new tab for DRM-free audiobooks. M4B and M4A books with their chapters, MP3 books (one file with chapters, or a folder of parts), FLAC, Ogg and WAV. Libro.fm's downloads play as they are: tap Add and choose its zip of MP3s (it's unzipped for you) or its M4B. Books in the phone's Audiobooks folder show up by themselves. Each book remembers where you were, Continue Listening puts the one you're in at the top, and touching and holding a book plays it from the start, marks it finished or removes one you added.
• Now Playing for audiobooks: the chapter as the title, Chapter 4 of 9 under it, skip back and ahead 15 seconds, and Chapters in place of Up Next: tap one to go there.
• Buy from Libro.fm: the Audiobooks tab suggests Libro.fm, whose audiobooks are DRM-free and support a local bookstore with every purchase.
• Playback speed: 0.8× to 2× with the voice at its own pitch, set separately for music, podcasts and audiobooks (Speed in Now Playing). The time left counts down at the speed you listen.
• Lyrics timing: if a song's lyrics run ahead or behind, tap Timing in Lyrics and move them half a second earlier or later. Grapefruit remembers it for that song.
• Podcast subscriptions in and out: Podcasts › Subscriptions › Import and Export, with an OPML file from or for another podcast app.
• Tab menus: tap the tab you're on, or touch and hold any tab but Settings, for its sections, sources and searches. Search's menu picks what to search.
• Library is now Music, and Recently Added shows only music and playlists.
• TV & Movies: if Grapefruit TV & Movies (or Grapefruit TV) is installed, a TV & Movies tab opens it.
• A new name and icon: the grapefruit now wears headphones, on the launcher, the splash screen and the About page, and the name is on two lines.
• The APK is now called grapefruit-audio-6.0-beta-1.apk.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository. It updates Grapefruit Music & Podcasts in place: your music, podcasts and settings stay.
