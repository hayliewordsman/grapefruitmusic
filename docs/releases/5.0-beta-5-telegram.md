🍊 Grapefruit Music & Podcasts 5.0 beta 5

• Library's chips stay put. Recently Added, Playlists, Artists, Albums and Songs now switch the page below the chips instead of opening a new one, so you can jump between them from anywhere. Each one remembers where you were scrolled.
• Podcasts works the same way: Discover, Subscriptions and Downloads.
• Back (or the first chip) takes you to Recently Added or Discover.
• On a phone turned sideways or a tablet, the title and chips stay in a column on the left while the music scrolls beside them.
• The music picker (On This Device, Downloads or your servers) is now a plain pill with an arrow at the start of the chips, so the accent only marks where you are.

Beta: GitHub prerelease only, not in the F-Droid repository.
